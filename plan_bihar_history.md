## 1. What Makes These Different

Unlike Lane K topics (24–149) that require fresh research, topics 155–158 are **Lane C consolidation pages**. Their content already exists — scattered across 8+ built topics as "Bihar Connection" sections. The job is to:

1. **Gather** the Bihar-specific facts from source topics into one place
2. **Add value** through a Bihar-only lens (site-to-district mapping, exam-focused fact matrix)
3. **Cross-link** back to the source topics rather than re-explain
4. **Drill** with a fresh 25-MCQ set per topic that tests Bihar angles specifically

The existing topics explain *why* Magadha rose or *how* Sher Shah reformed revenue. The Bihar History pages answer: **"Where in Bihar did this happen, and what BPSC questions come from it?"**

## 8. Anti-Duplication Rules

1. **NEVER copy full paragraphs** from source topics. Use condensed fact-matrix tables and cross-link.
2. **NEVER re-explain concepts** already covered in source topics. Link to them: "→ See Topic 100 for the six reasons for Magadha's rise."
3. **The Bihar History page adds value by being Bihar-ONLY.** Source topics cover India-wide context; Bihar History pages filter to what happened in Bihar's districts.
4. **MCQs must be fresh** — no question reused from source topic sidecars. Use Bihar-specific angles (site↔district matching, Bihar formation dates, Bihar leaders).
5. **Bihar Connection section is the whole page** — for these topics, §4 is not a separate angle but the organizing principle. Use it to go deeper on Bihar significance rather than restate §2.
