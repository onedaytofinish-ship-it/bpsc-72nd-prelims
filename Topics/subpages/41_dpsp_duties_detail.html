3️⃣ Background & Bihar Connection
Bihar's DPSP implementations (scenario bank)
  • Art 47 — prohibition: Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act 2016 — India's strictest; 2024–25 HC rulings on seizure/procedure keep it live. BPSC scenario favourite.
  • Art 40 — panchayats: Bihar's three-tier PRI system post-1993 Act; Karpoori Thakur's 1978 panchayat reforms pre-dated it.
  • Art 46 — weaker sections: Bihar's caste-survey (2023) based schemes (student scholarships, land grants) — modern Art 46 in action (links Topic 21).
  • Art 39 — land reforms: Bihar Land Reforms Act 1950 (zamindari abolition) — among India's earliest, though implementation saw later struggles (Bakasht movement history, Topic 26).
  • Art 48A — environment: Valmiki Tiger Reserve, Ganga dolphin, Budhni wetlands programmes.
4️⃣ Current Relevance & 7 Exam Predictions
  • UCC: Uttarakhand UCC 2024 (first state) — Art 44 is now a live legislation topic, not just theory.
  • POSHAN & living wage: 2024–25 wage-code implementation connects Arts 43 & 47.
  • FD revival in judgments: climate, public-property and scientific-temper citations (2024–25).
