2️⃣ The Election Commission's saga — T.N. Seshan to 2023
  • 1950: single-member EC (Sukumar Sen — first CEC).
  • 1989: brief two-member (controversial), reversed.
  • 1991: SC's quashed appointment case (Anang... the famous interim judgment path) → 1993 ordinance → 1996 Act — CEC + ECs parity for removal until 1995 (SC judgment — asymmetry restored).
  • T.N. Seshan (1990-96): MCC enforcement, voter-ID cards, symbols order — the EC's plenary-power era.
  • Anoop Baranwal (2023): appointments committee (PM+LoP+CJI) as interim measure.
  • 2023 Act: PM + LoP + Cabinet Minister (CJI out); search committee under it; first appointments (2024).
  • 2024 EVM-VVPAT verdict: SC declined 100% VVPAT counting; audit-verified stats published — trust-architecture current affairs.
BPSC Pattern Numbers to remember: EC = CEC + 2 ECs currently; tenure 6 years (or 65); removal asymmetry (324(5)); expenses charged on CF; MCC from announcement to results.

4️⃣ 89th/102nd Amendment — the commission splits
  • Pre-2003: one combined SC/ST Commission (Art 338).
  • 89th (2003): NCSC (338) + NCST (338A) from 2004 — separate chairs/members; duties expanded.
  • NCBC's journey: statutory (1993) → 123rd Amdt bill fail (2017 RS) → 102nd Amdt (2018): Art 338B constitutional + 342A state OBC lists (Maratha verdict's context; 105th Amdt 2021 restored states' SEBC power).
  • Minorities: Art 350B's Special Officer (constitutional) vs the NC Minorities (statutory, 1992) — a fine distinction BPSC can exploit.
  • Bihar link: EWS/OBC quota litigation, the 2015 SC-notification quash (70th Q98 — "Bhogta" community case) — the commissions' Bihar case-files.
