3️⃣ Background & Bihar Connection
Bihar & Fundamental Rights — enforcement stories
  • Art 21 & liquor-policy litigation: Bihar's prohibition regime (2016–) generated landmark HC/SC rulings on personal liberty, search-and-seizure, and due process under Art 21 — a live BPSC scenario bank.
  • Art 19(1)(g): Bihar prohibition also tested trade/business restrictions (liquor dealers' compensation cases) — the "complete exclusion" clause of 19(6) in action.
  • Art 17's Bihar lineage: Karpoori Thakur's 1978 OBC quota (first in India), Champaran's anti-indenture legacy, and the state's PCR Act enforcement history.
  • Art 23 & bonded labour: Bihar migrant workers' cases shaped bonded-labour jurisprudence (Bandhua Mukti Morcha line of SC cases draws on inter-state flows incl. Bihar).
  • Art 30: Bihar's minority-institution network (e.g., Patna's historic madrasas/mission schools) illustrates the state's composite culture.
4️⃣ Current Relevance & 7 Exam Predictions
  • Puttaswamy's continuing expansion: digital-rights, AI-surveillance, and data-protection litigation (DPDP Act 2023 challenges) keep Art 21 interpretation live.
  • EWS quota implementation cases (2024–25): post-Janhit Abhiyan follow-ups on income criteria.
  • Art 15(5)/RTE and minority-institution litigation: the 2022 AMU vs Naresh Agarwal ruling and follow-up debates.
