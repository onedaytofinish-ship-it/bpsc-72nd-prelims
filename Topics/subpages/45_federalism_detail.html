3️⃣ Fiscal Federalism — the full map
  • Constitutional articles: 268-293 (distribution + grants); Art 280 (FC); 275 (grants-in-aid); 282 (discretionary grants); 292-293 (borrowing).
  • 15th FC (2021-26): 41% vertical share; horizontal formula — income distance 45%, population 15%, area 15%, demographic performance 12.5%, forest & ecology 10%, tax effort 2.5% (sums to 100).
  • Grants: revenue-deficit grants, local-body grants (₹4.36 lakh crore), sector-specific (health/education), state-specific (Bihar's flood/caste-survey support)
  • Bihar's position: ~10% devolution (highest income distance), plus revenue-deficit grants, disaster-relief norms (floods), and CSS share — the "special-category demand" is about plan assistance beyond statutory devolution.
  • GST's fiscal revolution: 101st Amdt (2016): Art 246A, 269A, 279A; compensation cess (5 years, ended 2022; extension debates); "consensus chamber" federalism.
4️⃣ Asymmetric Federalism — special provisions
  • Art 371 series (A-J): Maharashtra-Gujarat (371), Nagaland (371A), Assam (371B), Manipur (371C), Sikkim (371F), Arunachal (371H), Goa (371I), Karnataka (371J) — asymmetric protections.
  • 5th Schedule: Scheduled areas (10 states incl. Jharkhand's neighbourhood — Bihar itself has no Scheduled areas post-2000 bifurcation).
  • 6th Schedule: tribal areas of Assam/Meghalaya/Tripura/Mizoram (ADCs) — 70th BPSC Q76-77 tested the state-set.
  • Art 370's saga: 1947-2019 special status → 2019 abrogation → Dec 2023 SC verdict (upholding; statehood promise; Ladakh's 6th-Schedule demand).
  • NCT Delhi (Art 239AA): 69th Amdt; services-tussle verdict (2023 Constitution Bench — services under Delhi govt except police/public order/land).
Bihar's federal milestones — rapid revision
  • 1912 separation: linguistic-province pioneer (pre-1956 linguistic reorganisation era).
  • 1950-51 Land Reforms: 9th Schedule's creation trigger (1st Amdt).
  • 2000 bifurcation: Jharkhand carved out (Bihar Reorganisation Act 2000) — mineral-base loss → special-category campaign.
  • 2005-06 Rameshwar Prasad: Governor-jurisprudence landmark.
  • 2015/2023-25 special packages: ₹1.25 lakh crore (2015) + ₹58,900 crore (2023-24 approvals) — cooperative federalism's Bihar arithmetic.
  • 2024-25: Nitish's coalition-era special-status revival; flood-relief Centre-state cost-sharing; caste-survey's policy federalism.
