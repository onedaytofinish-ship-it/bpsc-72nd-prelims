Assembly working — numbers that BPSC loves

  • 11 sessions; total time 2 years, 11 months, 18 days; cost ₹64 lakh.
  • Only 211 of 389 attended the first sitting (9 Dec 1946) due to the Muslim League boycott.
  • 7,632 amendments tabled; 2,473 discussed; the draft was considered clause-by-clause over 114 days.
  • The Assembly also functioned as the Dominion Legislature (1947–49) and then the Provisional Parliament (1950–52) — legislative and constitution-making roles overlapped.
  • Signatures: 284 members signed the final copy on 24 Jan 1950; the calligraphed original (Prem Behari Narain Raizada) is kept in helium cases in Parliament's library.

3️⃣ Background & Bihar Connection
Bihar in the making of the Constitution
  • Both Presidents of the Assembly were Biharis: temporary — Sachchidananda Sinha (Arrah); permanent — Rajendra Prasad (Siwan). BPSC has tested "who presided over the first meeting" — Sinha is the answer, Prasad is the trap.
  • Bihar's CA members included Sri Krishna Singh, Anugrah Narayan Sinha, Jagjivan Ram, Kameshwar Singh (Darbhanga), Binodanand Jha — the state's post-1937 political establishment.
  • Dr. Rajendra Prasad went on to be the first President of India (1950–62) — the only person to chair both the Assembly and the Republic's early governments.
  • Constitution's Bihar legacies: Art 40 panchayats ↔ Bihar's later panchayati tradition (Topic 48); the 5th Schedule ↔ Bihar's tribal areas (Topic 90); Bihar's separation (1912) preceded the provincial assembly that elected its CA members.
4️⃣ Current Relevance (2024–26) & 7 Exam Predictions
  • 75 years of adoption (26 Nov 2024 onwards) — Samvidhan Divas campaigns; "Hamra Samvidhan Hamra Swabhiman" drives across states including Bihar.
  • Samvidhan Sadan renaming (2023) of the old Parliament House, where the Assembly first sat.
  • SC on Preamble words (Nov 2024): refused to remove "socialist"/"secular" (42nd Amendment, 1976) — bridges to Topic 39.
