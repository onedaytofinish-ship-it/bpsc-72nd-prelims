Objectives Resolution → Preamble: the text evolution

  • 13 Dec 1946: Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution — "India shall be a Union of British India States & Indian States…".
  • 22 Jan 1947: adopted unanimously; it declared India an "Independent Sovereign Republic" and promised justice/liberty/equality/fraternity.
  • 26 Nov 1949: modified into the Preamble text and adopted with the Constitution; "SOVEREIGN DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC" phrasing finalised.
  • 1976 (42nd Amendment): "SOCIALIST", "SECULAR", "INTEGRITY" inserted — the sole textual change ever.

Mnemonic — case ladder: B-K-L-B: Berubari (said no) → Kesavananda (said yes) → LIC (re-confirmed) → Balram (kept the words). Note the irony: Berubari came first but is the OUTLIER view today.

3️⃣ Background & Bihar Connection
Bihar & the Preamble
  • Rajendra Prasad presided over the adoption of the Constitution (with its Preamble) on 26 Nov 1949 — Bihar's direct hand in the text's final approval.
  • "Economic justice" operationalised early in Bihar: Bihar was among the first states to legislate zamindari abolition (Bihar Land Reforms Act, 1950) — the Preamble's "economic justice" becoming statute within months of commencement.
  • Champaran (1917) — the "social/economic justice for tillers" theme that later echoed in the Preamble's justice clause.
  • Secular-state exemplars from Bihar: Maner Sharif & Phulwari Sharif (Sufi), Bodh Gaya & Pawapuri (Buddhist/Jain), Patna Sahib (Sikh) — useful illustrations for "positive secularism" answers.
