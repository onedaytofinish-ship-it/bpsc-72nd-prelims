Parliament — LS & RS, Lawmaking, Bills, Sessions, Committees

📚 Core Content & PYQ Alignment
71st BPSC PYQ Secretarial staff & officials (71st, Q56): BPSC asked: "The power to recruit the Secretarial staff of the House of the People is vested in:" — the Speaker (Lok Sabha's secretariat, Art 98; the Speaker controls the LS Secretariat, appoints its staff). Also expect questions on the Leader of the House (PM), and who presides when the Speaker is absent (Deputy Speaker).
BPSC Pattern Bill-passing arithmetic: BPSC repeatedly tests Money Bill certification (Art 110 — Speaker's decision final), joint-sitting mechanics (Art 108 — ordinary bills only), RS's special-majority empowerments (Arts 249/312), and no-confidence vs censure motions. The censure-vs-no-confidence distinction appeared in the 71st (Q71) — censure NEED NOT be against the whole CoM and does NOT require resignation if passed.

Parliament's architecture (Arts 79–122)

  • Art 79: Parliament = President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha (the President is integral yet not a member).
  • Lok Sabha (Arts 81–85): max 530 states + 20 UTs + up to 2 nominated Anglo-Indians (ended 2020 by 104th Amendment — now 530+20=550); directly elected, 18+ vote; 5-year term (dissolvable); quorum 1/10th.
  • Rajya Sabha (Arts 80–83): max 238 elected + 12 nominated = 250; permanent house, not subject to dissolution; 1/3 retire every 2 years; 6-year terms.
  • RS is NOT a federal equal: US-style equality absent — LS dominates money matters; RS's special powers: Art 249 (2/3 vote to legislate on State List for 1 year) & Art 312 (new all-India services by resolution).
  • Presiding officers: Speaker + Deputy Speaker (LS, elected from members); VP (RS Chairman) + Deputy Chairman; Speaker pro tem (swears in new LS); Speaker's casting vote only on a tie; Speaker does NOT vote in the first instance.
  • Leader of the House: PM (LS) / a minister (RS); Leader of Opposition — statutory post (1977 Act), largest opposition party with ≥10% LS seats.
Parliament House — Art 79: President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha. The new building (Samvidhan Sadan complex) has hosted sittings since Sept 2023.

Law-making flow (ordinary bill)

First Reading (introduction; no debate) → Second Reading (general discussion → committee stage → consideration clause-by-clause)
Third Reading (pass/reject — simple majority)
Other House repeats; disagreement → joint sitting (Art 108; Speaker presides; simple majority decides)
President's assent (Art 111) → Act

Committees — the workhorses

  • Public Accounts Committee (22): examines CAG reports; chair from opposition (convention since 1967); ministers excluded.
  • Estimates Committee (30): LS-only; examines the Budget's "economy" side; chair from ruling party.
  • Committee on Public Undertakings (22): PSU performance; ministers excluded.
  • Departmentally Related Standing Committees (DRSCs, 24): examine demands for grants, bills, annual reports; 31 members each (21 LS + 10 RS).
  • Others: Privileges, Petitions, Government Assurances, Subordinate Legislation, Absence of Members, Ethics, Empowerment of Women, JPC on Offices of Profit, Business Advisory, Rules.

✍️ MCQ Practice Set — 25 Questions (BPSC Level)
Q1. Under Article 79, the Parliament of India consists of:
✅ Correct Answer: (C) — Art 79: Parliament = President + RS + LS. Though the President is not a member of either House, no bill becomes law without presidential assent, and the President summons/prorogues Houses.
Q2. [71st BPSC] The power to recruit the Secretarial staff of the Lok Sabha is vested in:
✅ Correct Answer: (C) — The Speaker controls the Lok Sabha Secretariat (Art 98) — recruitment of LS secretarial staff vests in the Speaker. The 71st BPSC's distractors included the PMO and UPSC — wrong channels for House staff.
Q3. The Rajya Sabha is a permanent House because:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) — RS is permanent (Art 83): no dissolution; members serve 6-year terms, with 1/3 retiring biennially. Option D confuses individual term with the House's continuity.
Q4. A Money Bill can be introduced only:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) — Art 109/110/117(1): Money Bill only in the Lok Sabha, and only on the President's prior recommendation. RS cannot introduce, amend or reject it — just 14 days of recommendations.
Q5. Whose decision on whether a bill is a Money Bill is final?
✅ Correct Answer: (D) — Art 110(3): the Speaker's certification is final. Courts can examine procedural aspects (Aadhaar ruling 2018 tested but respected the certificate's scope) — the exam answer remains the Speaker.
Q6. A joint sitting of Parliament can be summoned for:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — Art 108 joint sitting: ordinary + Financial Bills (I/II) only. Excluded: Money Bills (RS can't deadlock them) and Constitution Amendment Bills (both Houses must pass identically by special majority). Only 3 joint sittings ever: 1961, 1978, 2002.
Q7. Who presides over a joint sitting of Parliament?
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — Art 108(2): the Speaker presides (Deputy Speaker next; then RS Deputy Chairman; never the VP — he's RS Chairman and cannot preside over a joint sitting). Called by the President.
Q8. [71st BPSC] Which is correct about a Censure Motion?
✅ Correct Answer: (C) — 71st BPSC answer: censure ≠ no-confidence. Censure targets a specific minister/act (or the CoM); it need not compel resignation (individual-censure), whereas no-confidence is against the whole CoM and forces resignation if passed.
Q9. The Rajya Sabha can authorise Parliament to legislate on a State List subject (Art 249) by:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) — Art 249: RS resolution by 2/3 present & voting (national interest) → Parliament legislates on State List for 1 year (renewable). Art 312 (all-India services) uses the same 2/3. LS cannot pass these resolutions — RS's two special powers.
Q10. The maximum strength of the Lok Sabha provided by the Constitution (post-104th Amendment) is:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) — After the 104th Amendment (2019, effective 2020) ended Anglo-Indian nomination, the ceiling = 530 states + 20 UTs = 550. (552 was the old ceiling with 2 nominated; 543 = current actual LS seats.)
Q11. The Public Accounts Committee of Parliament is chaired (by convention since 1967) by a member of:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — PAC (22 members: 15 LS + 7 RS) chairmanship goes to an opposition member since 1967; ministers cannot be members. PAC examines CAG reports — the CAG's audit (Topic 47) feeds it.
Q12. The quorum required to conduct business in either House of Parliament is:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) — Art 100(3): quorum = 1/10 of total membership (incl. presiding officer). The President can direct adjournment/suspension when quorum fails.
Q13. A no-confidence motion in the Lok Sabha requires the support of at least:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) — Leave for a no-confidence motion needs 50 members' support (Rules of Procedure). Adjournment motions also need 50. If passed, the CoM must resign (Art 75(3) consequence).
Q14. Which of the following is NOT a Money Bill matter under Article 110?
✅ Correct Answer: (C) — Art 110's 7 heads include taxes, borrowing, CF/CAF custody, appropriation, receipts, incidental. Art 274's "regulation of state taxes in public interest" is a financial bill requiring President's recommendation — but a draft of which a Money Bill definition excludes — the classic MCQ fine-print: regulation of state taxes is NOT an Art 110 head.
Q15. The Speaker of the Lok Sabha:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — Art 99/100: the Speaker votes only in a tie (casting vote). The RS Chairman (VP) cannot vote at all except an original vote on RS resolutions — a subtle LS/RS contrast.
Q16. The Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha is elected by:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — Art 89: RS elects its Deputy Chairman from its members; presides in the Chairman's absence. Deputy Speaker (LS) likewise elected by LS (Art 93).
Q17. The Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha is a statutory position recognised provided the largest opposition party has at least:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) — The Leader and Deputy Leader of Opposition (Salary) Act 1977: recognition requires ≥ 1/10 of total LS seats (currently ~54-55 seats — options C/D are number-traps of the same idea; the statutory test is the fraction).
Q18. Which committee examines the reports of the Comptroller and Auditor General?
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — The PAC examines CAG audit reports (appropriation accounts + finance accounts). Estimates suggests economies; CoPU examines PSU performance (also based on CAG reports, but PAC is the audit-report committee par excellence).
Q19. Under Article 312, a new all-India service can be created by:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) — Art 312: RS resolution (2/3 present & voting) declares necessity → Parliament enacts. Existing all-India services: IAS, IPS, IFoS — and the Indian Forest Service was created via this route (1966).
Q20. The annual financial statement (Budget) is laid before Parliament under Article:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) — Art 112: Annual Financial Statement (Budget). Art 114 = Appropriation Bill (after demands for grants); Art 117 = financial bill procedures; Art 110 defines Money Bills. The President causes it to be laid.
Q21. The anti-defection law is contained in:
✅ Correct Answer: (C) — 10th Schedule (52nd Amendment, 1985): disqualification on defection; Speaker/Chairman decide — subject to judicial review (Kihoto Hollohan, 1992).
Q22. A bill pending in the Lok Sabha lapses on its dissolution, but a bill pending in the Rajya Sabha:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) — A bill originating in and passed by LS, pending in RS at LS dissolution, does not lapse (RS continuity) — but a bill originating in RS and passed by it, pending in LS, lapses on LS dissolution. Lapse rules are a BPSC staple.
Q23. The Speaker Pro Tem is:
✅ Correct Answer: (C) — The President appoints the senior-most member as Speaker Pro Tem to swear in the new LS and preside until the Speaker's election. Options B/C are conventional confusions — the outgoing Speaker returns to the House as a member; the Dy Speaker ceases with dissolution.
Q24. The Women's Reservation (106th) Amendment, 2023, provides:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) — Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (Sept 2023): 1/3 reservation in LS + state assemblies + Delhi, taking effect after the post-census delimitation — the deferred-implementation clause is the exam point. RS nomination is not covered; it applies to direct elections.
Q25. Which one of the following is a Rajya Sabha-exclusive power (not available to the Lok Sabha)?
✅ Correct Answer: (B) — Art 312 (and 249) resolutions belong to the RS only. Option A: VP removal starts in RS but needs LS agreement — so it's not "exclusive"; Money Bills & no-confidence are LS domains. Bihar angle: all-India services include Bihar-recruited IAS/IPS cadres.
