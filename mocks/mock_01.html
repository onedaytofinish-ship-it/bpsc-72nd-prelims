4Sciencetopic 70
The functional unit of the human kidney (the basic filtering unit) is called:
Correct: (A) — The nephron is the functional unit of the kidney. Each kidney has ~1 million nephrons. A nephron consists of: (1) glomerulus (ball of capillaries where blood is filtered), (2) Bowman's capsule (collects filtrate), (3) proximal convoluted tubule, (4) Loop of Henle, (5) distal convoluted tubule, (6) collecting duct. The nephron filters blood, reabsorbs useful substances (water, glucose, amino acids), and excretes waste (urea, excess salts) as urine. Kidneys filter ~180 litres of blood per day, producing ~1.5 litres of urine.

8Sciencetopic 70
How many bones are there in the adult human body?
Correct: (D) — The adult human body has 206 bones. At birth, humans have ~270 bones — but many fuse together during growth and development, resulting in 206 bones in adults. The skeleton is divided into: (1) axial skeleton (80 bones — skull, vertebral column, rib cage) and (2) appendicular skeleton (126 bones — limbs, pectoral girdle, pelvic girdle). The smallest bone is the stapes (in the middle ear, ~3mm). The largest bone is the femur (thigh bone). Bones provide structure, protect organs, produce blood cells (in bone marrow), and store minerals (calcium, phosphorus).

21Historytopic 101
The Buddha and Mahavira were contemporaries. Which statement about them is correct?

36Sciencetopic 69
Which type of cell division produces two identical daughter cells with the same number of chromosomes as the parent cell?
Correct: (B) — Mitosis is the type of cell division that produces two identical daughter cells, each with the same number of chromosomes as the parent cell (diploid → 2 diploid). 59Sciencetopic 69
Lysosomes are called the 'suicide bags' of the cell because:
Correct: (D) — Lysosomes are called 'suicide bags' (or 'digestive bags') because they contain powerful digestive enzymes (hydrolases) that can break down cellular waste, damaged organelles, and even the cell itself. When a cell is damaged or old, lysosomes can rupture and release their enzymes — digesting the cell from within (autolysis). This is a form of programmed cell death. Lysosomes are produced by the Golgi apparatus. They are found mainly in animal cells (rare in plant cells). Christian de Duve discovered lysosomes (1955).

In the human digestive system, where does most of the nutrient absorption take place?
Correct: (C) — The small intestine is where ~90% of nutrient absorption occurs. Its inner surface is lined with villi (finger-like projections) and microvilli (tiny hair-like structures on villi cells) — which increase the surface area for absorption by ~600-fold. The Iqta system of the Delhi Sultanate was:
Correct: (B) — The Iqta system was a system of land revenue assignments (iqtas) to military commanders (iqtadars), who were responsible for: (1) collecting revenue from their iqtas; (2) maintaining troops for the Sultan; (3) maintaining law and order.
